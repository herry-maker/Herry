Branding gaps to fix (critical review of the 2022 profile)

  1. Four names for one company. The legal name is Kreasi Prima Printing, the website is kreasipercetakan.com, Instagram is percetakankreasi and Facebook is Percetakan Limatujuh. A buyer who searches one name will not find the others. Pick one public name (for example "KPP – Kreasi Prima Printing") and align handles, Google Business Profile and marketplace shop names to it.
  2. The profile sells machines, not outcomes. Most pages show equipment. Corporate buyers care about results: brand consistency, on-time delivery, colour accuracy. Lead with client outcomes and case studies; keep machinery as supporting proof.
  3. Branding is your selling point but is missing from the profile. There is no page on design or brand services. Add one built around the design service levels in this system (Basic, Standard, Premium) and the kits.
  4. No offer architecture. The profile has no minimum order quantities, lead times or starting prices. Add "from" prices for core items using the Price list tab, so prospects can self-qualify.
  5. Tagline under-used. "We Make Great Impressions!" is a strong double meaning for a printer and brander. Put it on the cover, quotes, packaging tape and staff T-shirts.
  6. Dated material. The profile is from 2022 and has spelling errors (for example "kepiuasan", "Latter Box", "Acrilic"). Refresh it yearly; for a company that sells print quality, typos cost credibility.

How the pricing system stays fast and accurate

One source of truth. All costs live in the Cost library. Nobody prices from memory or old WhatsApp chats. One named person updates supplier costs at least monthly and whenever a supplier changes a rate.

Cost-plus floor, value-based top. The engine guarantees cost recovery and a target margin. Your branding work is the value you sell, so it appears as a visible service level (design fee) rather than hidden in the unit price. That lets you defend the price and upsell from Basic to Premium.

Approval by exception. Staff may quote freely at or above target. A quote between target and the floor needs a written reason. A quote below the floor needs owner approval. The status pill tells staff which case they are in.

Speed rule. Standard items are quoted straight from the Price list tab during the first WhatsApp or call. Custom jobs and kits use the builders; target turnaround is the same working day.
