Statistics of the current 4096-sample window

Mean
—
Average sample value: the DC offset or baseline. Audio should sit near 0; a steady offset points to the microphone or interface.
Std dev · variance
—
Spread of the samples about the mean. Steady sounds (a hum) hold it still; speech, music and traffic make it swing.
Skewness
—
Asymmetry of the amplitude distribution. Near 0 for most tones; positive or negative when one polarity carries the peaks.
Kurtosis (excess)
—
Peakedness and outliers. 0 is Gaussian noise, −1.5 a pure sine; high values mean sharp bursts, clicks or impulsive noise.
Zero-crossing rate
—
Sign changes per second. Low for clean low tones, high for noisy or percussive sounds; about 2 × f0 for a pure tone.
RMS amplitude
—
Root mean square: the effective level over the window, shown as a fraction of full scale and in dBFS.
Peak · crest factor
—
Peak over RMS in dB. A sine gives 3 dB; plucked, struck and spoken sounds give 10 to 20 dB.
Reading
—
A plain-word summary of the measures above.

  • Nothing is detected from any source? Run Self-test tone. If it reads the chosen note, the detector works and the problem is the input path (microphone, permission or level). If it does not, reload the page and check last error above.
  • Microphone button does nothing or says blocked: the page must be opened over https:// or localhost, in a real browser tab (not an in-app browser or an iframe without microphone permission). Allow the microphone in the address bar and in the operating system's privacy settings.
  • Input stays at −∞ dB: the browser is receiving silence. Another app may hold the microphone, the wrong device may be selected (use the device list that appears after permission), or the system input is muted.
  • Level moves but no note appears: the sound is not periodic enough. Move closer, sustain the note, lower the Sensitivity to Sensitive, or pick a narrower Range.
  • Wrong octave: narrow the Range to the instrument; strong second harmonics in bass notes can fool any detector when the band is wide open.
