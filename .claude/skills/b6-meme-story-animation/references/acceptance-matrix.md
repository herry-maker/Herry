# B6 acceptance: 8 groups, 35 subchecks, three separate decisions

Condensed from `references/b6-sample-1-acceptance.md` and
`b6-motion-review.md` in kaomei/stickman-video-director (MIT). IDs match
review schema v3 as generated by `review-pack`; `style-standard.json`
lists the eight `required_checks`.

## Three decisions that never substitute for each other

1. **Technical checks** (runtime): input hashes, encode, limb/contact inventories, dimensions, caption contract, resource audit. Establish constraints only.
2. **Motion/visual review** (the agent, ideally an independent reviewing agent given brief + reference ranges + plan + rendered segment, without the author's verdict): every subcheck below gets its own observation from ACTUAL playback and decoded frames. `pending` / `needs_repair` keeps the task active; it is a work product, not a reason to wait for the user.
3. **User decision** on that exact preview and ratio. Never inferred from 1 or 2; never inferred across ratios.

`preserved` ≠ `matched`: an unchanged hash proves reuse, not that timing, acting or captions match Sample 1. No averaging or score cancels a visible defect. `review_correction` is only for a genuine prior misreport; a confirmed visible defect (`confirmed_defect: true`) closes only through changed source + changed rendered pixels.

## Evidence rules

- Compare candidate vs Sample 1 portrait V44 at matched head width, comparable views, original body proportions; record frame paths, times and normalization.
- Inspect full native-size encoded crops, complete limb chains for every identity/view/near-far arm, contacts frame by frame, both sides of every cut, and full-range original-speed playback WITH sound (`full_playback` names who watched).
- Caption evidence must include active lettered cue frames plus the frames before/after a cue switch, for candidate and reference.
- `cast_inventory` in the review must equal the native plan's declared identities and shot memberships inside the preview range, including painted-in background people.
- `not_applicable` exists only for `background_independent_activity` and `contact_ownership_release`, each with a concrete scene reason.
- Low-resolution contact sheets locate problems; they cannot pass detail, motion or audio.

## The 35 subchecks

### space_depth
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `world_routes_reveal` | Connected usable regions, dressed destinations, enough width/height for the real route; a move reveals new information, not an enlarged copy of one facade. |
| `depth_planes_parallax_occlusion` | Near/middle/far respond coherently to the claimed move; crossings and changing occlusion exist where "depth" is claimed. A flat reframe is described as a reframe. |
| `spatial_staging_scale` | Actor, prop, counter/floor, destination share believable scale and contact planes; environment-led or protagonist-free shots are valid. |
| `background_independent_activity` | Extras/transport have staggered, purposeful, independent activity; walk stance follows world travel, wheels follow distance/radius. N/A allowed for a genuinely quiet set. |

### camera_purpose
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `motivated_attention_landings` | Each move follows/reveals a subject and lands on useful information; a new shot ID or whole-canvas zoom is not new camera language. |
| `native_composition_safe_coverage` | The requested ratio has its own composition, readable face/hand/prop/caption relations, covered camera extremes; no crop/stretch of the other format. |
| `actor_similarity_no_warp` | Faces, torsos, limbs keep uniform actor scale and view; only the environment gains projection stretch. |
| `cut_axis_gaze_continuity` | Screen direction, eyelines, prop ownership and action phase carry across joins, or the change is established. |

### body_action
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `anticipation_action_recovery` | Preparation → decisive action/peak → release/recovery, timed for the story; a deliberate hold is valid, wrist-only posing is not. |
| `shoulder_weight_support` | Shoulder/torso/hip support the reach/turn/rise; planted feet or seat explain the weight. |
| `contact_ownership_release` | Every supported prop shares a hand transform through approach, shared hold, transfer, release, recovery, with correct near/far ownership and masks. N/A only with no hand-supported contact. |
| `stance_swing_or_deliberate_hold` | Holds are grounded; locomotion follows world distance with modest lift and heel/flat/toe support; the straighter-leg gait keeps its read. |

### expression_gaze
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `identity_shape_consistency` | Expression/view switches preserve head/hair silhouette, face placement, head/body paint. |
| `emotional_progression` | Emotion changes because of perceived events; brows/lids/pupils/mouth and body support the beat; no frozen smirk, no emoji shock. |
| `eyeline_target` | Eyes/head address a named partner, prop or event; gaze arrival and body action deliberately offset. |
| `reaction_readable_hold` | The reaction lands at an intelligible moment and has room to register. |

### rhythm_continuity
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `cause_action_reaction` | Setup, action/result, reaction/payoff are locatable without the plan explaining them. |
| `travel_brake_land_hold` | Purposeful departure, decisive transit, braking/landing, reading hold; actor and camera clocks independent. |
| `cut_readability_no_flash` | New text/props/places can be located and read; sub-0.9 s cuts have a recorded purpose; no single-frame inserts. |
| `focus_background_competition` | One primary attention cue leads; background life supports rather than fights it. |

### drawing_anatomy
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `irregular_ink_pressure` | Selective pressure changes, imperfect contours, logical breaks/repasses; reads drawn, not vector or noise-filtered. |
| `unified_head_body_paint` | Head, body, props, extras and scenery share a deliberate detail/paint hierarchy; texture anchored to artwork. |
| `single_stroke_fixed_limb_ownership` | Sparse single painted limbs, fixed chains, correct sleeve exits, near/far order and deliberate occlusion for every actor/view (`cast_coverage` lists each identity). |
| `lighting_bloom_material_depth` | Soft light, readable midtones, localized restrained spill; matte paint and spatial separation preserved. |
| `shadows_edges_no_plastic` | Pale colored underside/contact shadows, grounded shoes/props, soft transitions; no glossy highlights, crushed ink, halos, detached shadows. |

### limb_line_weight (cannot be N/A; needs candidate AND reference PNGs plus `comparison_basis`)
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `matched_head_width_primary_frames` | Candidate and Sample 1 frames use comparable views at matched head width with original proportions; frames, times and normalization recorded. |
| `relative_stroke_pressure` | Arm/leg weight, pressure variation and painted edges read like Sample 1 (visibly heavier than V90); no universal pixel width, no flesh-filled forearms. |
| `scale_pose_supporting_cast_consistency` | Relative stroke coherent across scales, views, poses and the whole cast; one hero close-up certifies nothing. |

### native_audio_captions
| ID | Required result |
|---|---|
| `caption_placement_typography` | Approved Archivo Black portrait profile (47 px, baseline 914, white/#171319, 44 px sides at 720×1280) bound to the source fingerprint; landscape has its own documented layout. |
| `caption_semantics_timing` | Complete ordered wording, meaning-based breaks, correct speech boundaries, readable holds, no overlaps. |
| `caption_raster_switch` | 3× supersampled text, clean hard phrase replacement; no per-cue shrink/pop/bounce/shadow/blank flash. |
| `actual_voice_sync_readability` | Words, accents and pauses agree with picture and cues at original speed; comfortable reading at final size. |
| `original_speed_voice_identity` | Accepted voice, words, timing and authorized reuse preserved; no rate/pitch change, no clipping. |
| `voice_music_foley_hierarchy` | Intelligible speech, story-specific ambience/contact/transition sounds, intentional quiet and payoff; `contains_sfx` metadata proves nothing. |
| `final_media_identity_av_playback` | The delivered file, ratio, full range, source/reference hashes and review evidence agree; continuous playback with sound, including the ending. |

## Stop-and-repair triggers (fix the responsible art/binding/timing before extending)

Limb weight drifting toward V90's thin wire; era conveyed only by a label/filter/old prop; mixed or anachronistic period cues; uniform clean vector outlines; head/body brush mismatch; round emoji faces; polished 3D shine; constantly frozen expression; duplicate/missing/floating/reversed limbs; sliding paper or feet; crouched gait or backward knees; camera replaced by actor sway or whole-frame wobble; repeated flat zoom on the same storefront; unmotivated sub-second flashes; caption pop/bounce/shrink or wrong baseline; time-stretched narration; doubled SFX; music or SFX without rights evidence.

Each repair record needs the actual change, a new rendered observation, candidate playback evidence and the fingerprinted implementation file. Re-encoding an unchanged scene closes nothing.
